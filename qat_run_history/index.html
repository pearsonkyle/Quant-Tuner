    QAT run history¶

    Generated by scripts/qat_registry.py — do not hand-edit; it is a join over run_config.json, train.log, eval/stop_prob.csv, the per-tag tool-call CSV and swe-mimic's results. Re-run it after any training or eval finishes.

    provenance says where the config came from: recorded (the trainer wrote it), backfilled (reconstructed by hand from the launch script — trust it less), or log-only (nothing but train.log was available).

    stop_p_sentence is P(<|im_end|>) after a completed sentence — vanilla ~0.009 is healthy, ~0.97 means the model stops mid-task. stop_p_after_tool is the control, where a HIGH value is correct.
